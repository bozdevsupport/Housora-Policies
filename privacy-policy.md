# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 14, 2026**

HomeLog ("we," "our," or "the app") is a home-inventory and maintenance-tracking app. This policy explains what information HomeLog collects, how it's used, and your choices.

## Information We Collect

**Account information.** HomeLog uses Sign in with Apple. Depending on your choice during sign-in, we receive either your real email address or an anonymous private relay address, plus a unique account identifier. We never see your Apple ID password.

**Content you create.** Homes, rooms, assets, photos, maintenance schedules, and warranty records you add to the app. This content is stored securely on our backend (Supabase) so it can sync across your devices.

**Household sharing.** If you invite someone to share a home, we store the email address you invite and, once accepted, that person's account identifier as a member of that home. Members you invite can see and edit everything in the shared home.

**Subscription information.** Purchases are processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit. We receive confirmation of your subscription tier but never see your payment details — Apple handles all billing.

## How We Use Information

We use the information above only to operate the app: syncing your data across devices, enabling household sharing you set up yourself, and unlocking features tied to your subscription tier. We do not use your information for advertising, and we do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

## Data Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except:

- **Supabase**, our backend provider, which stores your account and content data on our behalf.
- **Apple**, which processes Sign in with Apple authentication and subscription purchases.

Neither is used for advertising or tracking purposes, and HomeLog does not use tracking technologies to follow you across other companies' apps or websites.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Your content is retained as long as your account is active. To request deletion of your account and all associated data, contact us at the email below. Household sharing: if you leave or are removed from a shared home, you lose access to that home's content, but content you personally own is unaffected.

## Children's Privacy

HomeLog is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by an updated "Last updated" date above.
