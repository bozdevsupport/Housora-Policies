## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to pay to use Housora?**
No — Housora is free to use with one home. Housora Pro and Housora Pro for Hosts unlock more homes, cross-device sync, and household sharing.

**How do I share a home with my spouse or a co-host?**
Open a home's Sharing screen and invite them by email. They'll need to accept the invite from their own device — once accepted, they get full access to view and edit everything in that home.

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
Manage or cancel any time in **Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions** on your iPhone.

**I found a bug. Where do I report it?**
Email us at the address above with as much detail as you can — what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead.
